Inter-Faculty Debate Championship

Eight teams, one trophy. Each year the Inter-Faculty Debate Championship brings together student speakers from across the university for a full day of structured argument in front of a live audience and a panel of three judges.

Teams of three receive their motion thirty minutes before each round, so the emphasis is on quick thinking, clear structure and respectful rebuttal rather than rehearsed speeches. Quarter-finals run in the morning, semi-finals after lunch, and the final begins at 4:30 pm in the Main Auditorium.

Members can attend free of charge, and every attendee receives a feedback sheet that explains how the judges scored each round — a useful way to learn what makes an argument persuasive.

What to expect

Doors open at 9:00 am. Refreshments are available between rounds, and a networking hour follows the prize-giving.

Announcement

Early registration is open. Members who sign up before 31 October pay no joining fee for the autumn term.

Tip: Bring your student ID to the first session so we can issue your membership card on the spot.

Questions? Visit the society office or register online.
