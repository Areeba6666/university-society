Who We Are

The LGU Society is a student-run society at Lahore Garrison University that brings together people from every department. We started as a small debating circle and have grown into a community of more than 400 members who run weekly sessions, charity drives and an annual showcase.

Whether you want to sharpen your public speaking, learn a practical skill from a peer, or simply meet people outside your classroom, there is a place for you here. No experience is needed — only curiosity.

What We Do

Our calendar is built around six regular activities:

  • Weekly debates — friendly, moderated rounds on current and campus topics.
  • Skill workshops — hands-on sessions in design, writing, CV building and basic coding.
  • Community service — tutoring, book drives and neighbourhood clean-ups.
  • Guest talks — alumni and industry speakers share how they got started.
  • Photography walks — monthly outings to practise composition and storytelling.
  • Annual showcase — a spring evening of talks, performances and exhibitions.
